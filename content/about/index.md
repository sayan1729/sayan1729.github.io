I'm Sayan, a 4th year maths undergrad at [Jadavpur University](https://jadavpuruniversity.in/), primarily interested in number theory and arithmetic geometry. In particular, I'm interested in in automorphic forms, subconvexity of \(L\)-functions, equidistribution, sieve theory, elliptic curves and Iwasawa theory.

In July 2025 I was a [Summer Research Intern](isiant25/cert.pdf) at the ISI Kolkata supervised by [Prof. Satadal Ganguly](https://sites.google.com/site/satadalganguly/).

Lately I've been reading Rudin's [*Real & Complex Analysis*](https://www.mheducation.co.in/real-and-complex-analysis-9789355326119-india), Jacobson's [*Basic Algebra 2*](https://store.doverpublications.com/collections/math-algebra/products/9780486471877), Goldstein's [*Classical Mechanics*](https://www.amazon.in/-/hi/Classical-Mechanics-Goldstein-Herbert-Hardcover/dp/0201025108), Willard's [*General Topology*](https://store.doverpublications.com/products/9780486434797), Samuel's [*Algebraic Theory of Numbers*](https://store.doverpublications.com/products/9780486466668), Fr\(\"\text{o}\)hlich & Taylor's [*Algebraic Number Theory*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/algebraic-number-theory/A47B605B1066C5781C1466FF19E50160), and Atiyah & MacDonald's [*Introduction to Commutative Algebra*](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/mono/10.1201/9780429493638/introduction-commutative-algebra-michael-atiyah-macdonald).
