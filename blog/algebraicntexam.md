
# Algebraic Number Theory Exam

1. Let $d$ be square free and suppose $d = 2,3\pmod{4}.$ Consider the quadratic field $K = \mathbb{Q}(\theta)$ where $\theta = \sqrt{d},$ let $p$ be a prime number and $P$ a prime ideal of $\mathcal{O}_K$ which contains $p.$
    - Prove that
        - If $\alpha, \beta \in \mathcal{O}_K$ are such that $\alpha = \beta \mod{p}$ then $\alpha = \beta \mod{ P}.$
        - $(a+b\theta)^p = a+b\theta^p \mod{ P}.$
        - $\theta^p = \left(\dfrac{d}{p}\right) \theta \mod{ P}$ if $p$ is odd, $\theta^p = \theta \mod{ P}$ if $p = 2.$
    where $\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)$ is the Legendre symbol.
    - Using the prime ideal factorization of $p,$ prove $\alpha^{Nm(P)} = \alpha \mod{ P}.$

2. Let $p = 5 \mod{ 8}$ be a prime number and let $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2p}).$ Prove that:
    - If $P$ is a prime ideal s.t. $2$ is in $P$ then $P$ is not principal.
    - the ring of integers isn't a unique factorization domain.
    - $2,p, \sqrt{2p}$ are irreducible elements.

3. Consider $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{103}).$
    - Find all prime ideals of $\mathcal{O}_K$ with norm $\leqslant 11.$
    - Letting $P = \langle 2,1+\sqrt{103}\rangle $ and $Q = \langle 3, 1+ \sqrt{103}\rangle $ justify that $P$ and $Q$ are prime ideals and that $\langle 10+\sqrt{103}\rangle  = Q$ and $\langle 7 +\sqrt{103}\rangle  = PQ^3.$
    - using the previous question jusitfy that $P = \langle 7+\sqrt{103}\rangle Q^{-3} = \langle \alpha\rangle $ where $\alpha = (7+\sqrt{103})/(10 + \sqrt{103})^3$ is in $\mathcal{O}_K$ and conclude that $\epsilon = \alpha^2/2$ is a unit in $\mathcal{O}_K.$

4. Compute the class number $h(K)$ of $K = \mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-35}).$
