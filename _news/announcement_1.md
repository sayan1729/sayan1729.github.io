I am glad to share that I have successfully completed a summer internship in analytic number theory at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata under the supervision of Professor Satadal Ganguly, lasting from 1st July to 31st July, 2025.

---

During my internship I worked through Apostol's Introduction to Analytic Number Theory, and learnt about the distribution of primes, asymptotic estimates of arithmetic functions, Dirichlet characters and $L$-functions especially in connection with Dirichlet's Theorem on primes in arithmetic progression, Fourier series for periodic arithmetic functions, Ramanujan sums and Gauss sums in connection with quadratic reciprocity. And of course, the pinnacle of classical analytic number theory - the Prime Number Theorem (PNT).

The internship culminated with me learning about Selberg’s elementary proof of the PNT in Chebyshev form, which is remarkable for being the first proof of the PNT not requiring any complex analytic methods. The key to the proof is Selberg's asymptotic formula. My [internship report](https://sayan1729.github.io/assets/pdf/isiant25/selberg.pdf) is an exposition of Selberg's proof, and I also briefly mention a generalisation of the PNT to prime ideals in a number field.

Of course, I am immensely grateful to Prof. Ganguly for taking the time to help me learn such a beautiful and rich subject. I am looking forward to further expand upon the knowledge and skills that I have gained this summer and apply them in my future research and academic endeavours.
