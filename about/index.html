About me

I’m Sayan, a 4th year maths undergrad at Jadavpur University, primarily interested in number theory and arithmetic geometry. In particular, I’m interested in in automorphic forms, subconvexity of \(L\)-functions, equidistribution, sieve theory, elliptic curves and Iwasawa theory.

In July 2025 I was a Summer Research Intern at the ISI Kolkata supervised by Prof. Satadal Ganguly.

Lately I’ve been reading Rudin’s Real & Complex Analysis, Jacobson’s Basic Algebra 2, Goldstein’s Classical Mechanics, Willard’s General Topology, Samuel’s Algebraic Theory of Numbers, Fr\(\"\text{o}\)hlich & Taylor’s Algebraic Number Theory, and Atiyah & MacDonald’s Introduction to Commutative Algebra.
